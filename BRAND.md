# Breakwater Booking brand guide

Breakwater Booking is a selective, dependable live-booking and artist-representation company based in Southern California. The identity is coastal in structure, not in cliché: stable forms, open channels, clear routes, and deliberate signals.

The rendered visual reference lives at `/brand` and is intentionally excluded from search indexing.

## Positioning

**Primary descriptor:** Independent live booking and artist representation from Southern California.

**Primary headline:** Independent booking. Thoughtful representation.

**Brand promise:** Clear communication, complete details, and worthwhile live opportunities.

## Voice

Use concise sentences, concrete information, and calm confidence. Breakwater is selective, direct, informed, dependable, and specific.

Avoid inflated claims, vague superlatives, fake scale, industry theater, and ocean puns. Never use “making waves,” “ride the wave,” or similar copy.

## Identity

The symbol shows two offset masses forming a protected negative-space channel. A rust signal line passes through the opening. It may suggest a letter “B,” but it must remain abstract.

The primary lockup pairs the symbol with an uppercase Barlow Condensed wordmark. “Booking” is smaller and more widely tracked than “Breakwater.” Use the complete lockup unless space requires the symbol alone.

Do not add literal waves, anchors, palm trees, microphones, distressed effects, shadows, gradients, or outlines to the identity.

| Breakwater Ink | `#111A1D` | Primary type, dark fields, structural mass |
| Warm Foam | `#F2EEE5` | Primary background |
| Paper | `#FBF9F4` | Elevated surfaces and forms |
| Harbor Blue | `#23414A` | Deep secondary fields |
| Steel Tide | `#42666D` | Secondary type and section emphasis |
| Signal Rust | `#C95D35` | Actions, status, directional emphasis |
| Fog | `#A9B3AF` | Secondary rules and quiet information |

Primary combinations are Ink on Foam and Foam on Ink. Rust is an accent, never a large default background.

## Typography

- Display and wordmark: Barlow Condensed, weights 600–800.
- Body and interface: Inter, regular through bold.
- Headlines are usually uppercase with tight leading and slightly negative tracking.
- Labels use Inter bold, uppercase, with generous tracking.

## Layout and motion

Use editorial grids, strong horizontal rules, numbered sections, generous whitespace, and small route or coordinate details. Asymmetry is encouraged when the reading order remains obvious.

Motion should be slow, structural, and optional. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Avoid scroll hijacking, parallax, autoplay media, and decorative cursor effects.

## Photography

Breakwater does not depend on photography. The company hero remains image-free. Artist photography, when used later, should be documentary rather than promotional: real rooms, real performance, controlled contrast, accurate skin tones, and restrained grading.

Do not use generic music stock, AI-generated performers, nightclub neon, or artist photography as the Breakwater company identity.

## Accessibility

Maintain WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, descriptive labels, and useful alternative text. Brand expression never overrides legibility or interaction clarity.
